# Plattino

## Business Management & E-commerce Platform

Plattino is a modern business management and e-commerce platform designed to help businesses manage their operations, products, inventory, customers, suppliers, sales, and online commerce from a unified system.

## 🚀 Features

- 📦 Product and inventory management
- 🛒 E-commerce and online catalog
- 👥 Customer management
- 🏢 Supplier management
- 💰 Sales and financial management
- 🧾 Orders and transactions
- 🏪 Multi-store business management
- 📊 Business dashboards and reporting
- 🌐 Multilingual interface
- 🔐 Authentication and role-based access control

## 🛠️ Technology

- TypeScript
- React
- Node.js
- Express
- PostgreSQL
- Drizzle ORM
- Vite
- Tailwind CSS
